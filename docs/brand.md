# Headroom brand

Mood: a quiet instrument. Grey ink on the system material, colour only when a limit is in trouble.

- Type: SF Pro (system), tabular numbers everywhere a number can change.
- Colour: neutral fills. Amber means under 20% left, red means under 5% left. Green only for the live-session dot.
- Provider marks are two-letter monograms in a soft square. No brand logos.
- Motion: bars ease from zero when the panel opens, numbers roll when they change, the detail page slides in. Nothing loops except the live dot.
- Don't: gradients, glass for its own sake, emoji, more than one accent on screen at a time.
